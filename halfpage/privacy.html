Halfpage — Privacy policy

Draft. Halfpage is not yet released. Not reviewed by a lawyer.

This policy covers the Halfpage app and its web reader.

Effective date: to be confirmed before launch. Controller and contact: to be confirmed before launch. Australian privacy law applies.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in with an emailed code.
  • That you confirmed you are 18 or over. We don't ask for or store your date of birth.
  • Your diary entries and any photos you add. Photos are resized on your phone and their location and camera details are removed, then removed again on our server.
  • The names and email addresses of the people you add to your circle.
  • The letters you send, and which circle members they went to.
  • If you turn on "New letters" notifications, your phone's push token.
  • Reports you file about a letter and people you block.

What we don't collect

  • No contacts, no location, no advertising identifiers.
  • No record of whether anyone has opened or read a letter.
  • No analytics or advertising tools from other companies.

Who sees your writing

Only you, until you send a letter. A letter contains only the days you chose to include. It goes to the people you chose, by email, in the app, or on a private web link made for each person. Photos are not put in emails. Links expire after 90 days and stop working if you remove the person or revoke the letter. Emails already delivered stay in the recipient's inbox.

Who we share data with (service providers)

  • Our database, storage and sign-in host (Supabase, Sydney region).
  • Our email sender (Postmark or Resend, to be confirmed before launch).
  • Expo's push notification service, only if you turn on letter notifications. A push carries the author's name and nothing from inside the letter.

We don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI.

Your choices

  • Export: You, Settings, Export my data gives you a copy as a file.
  • Delete: You, Settings, Delete account removes your account, entries, photos, circle, letters and links, and wipes the copy on your phone.
  • Stop emails: every letter email has a stop link. Stopping applies to that author only.
  • Block or report an author from the ... menu on a letter. The author is not told.

Keeping data

Until you delete your account. Reports about other people are kept if the reported person deletes their account, with their identity cleared. How long deleted data remains in backups is to be confirmed before launch.

Children

The app is for people aged 18 and over. If you say you are under 18 at sign-up, you are signed out and nothing is saved.

Contact and complaints

To be confirmed before launch. email address. In Australia you can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
